Architecture Overview

The settings panel is a two-pane TUI embedded inside the Home screen. It is activated via Ctrl+S from chat, or by clicking the Settings tab.

Two-Pane Layout

Wide mode (≥80 cols): Left sidebar (25%, min 20, max 40 cols) shows grouped section list. Right content panel shows the active section. A 2-row hint bar runs across the bottom.

Narrow mode (<80 cols): Single pane with a section selector at the top.
